    THE IDEA

    Less footage.
    More useful context.

    Instead of asking a person to watch every second of a recording, this demonstration highlights a few moments for review. It runs in the browser using a general-purpose person detector.

    A prototype for discussion, not a safety system. Accuracy and reliability have not been validated for industrial deployment. No Axelera hardware or SDK was used.
