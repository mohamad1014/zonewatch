# ZoneWatch third-party materials

- The sample is an excerpt of Intel IoT DevKit's [worker-zone-detection.mp4](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos/blob/master/worker-zone-detection.mp4), from the [sample-videos repository](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos), under [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos/blob/master/LICENSE). It has been trimmed to 14 seconds, resized, converted to 15 frames per second, re-encoded, and has no audio. A copy of the license is at `assets/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt`.
- TensorFlow.js 4.22.0 and the COCO-SSD 2.2.3 wrapper are Apache-2.0 licensed; their license texts are in `vendor/`. The bundled `tf.min.js` has two substitutions: global-object and regenerator-runtime dynamic-`Function` fallbacks use `globalThis` instead, so the detector works under the website's existing script policy.
- The SSDLite MobileNet V2 detector files are the TensorFlow.js model distributed at <https://storage.googleapis.com/tfjs-models/savedmodel/ssdlite_mobilenet_v2/model.json>. The [TensorFlow.js model wrapper](https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/coco-ssd) identifies the source model family; [Intel's model catalog](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/open_model_zoo/blob/master/models/public/ssdlite_mobilenet_v2/model.yml) references the TensorFlow Models Apache-2.0 license for that pretrained model. See the [upstream licensing discussion](https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow/issues/27534) for the TensorFlow.js models repository. No rights or endorsement are claimed beyond the applicable upstream licenses.
